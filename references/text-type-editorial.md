# Text type: editorial

Open this file for news items, newsletters, blog posts and presentations – texts the reader chooses to read through rather than scan for an answer.
- Read with the audience rules in `SKILL.md` §3. Editorial texts can address any audience.
- Blog posts need nothing beyond `SKILL.md`: lead with the benefit (VOI-01), name the limits (VOI-05), keep the sentence length (MEC-01).

---

## 1 News items

**EDT-01 The first sentence carries the news, and the headline states the finding.** What happened, who it affects. Background follows, never precedes.
- ✅ *Ab sofort können Organisationen ihre digitale Kennung im Basisregister eintragen.* · 🚫 *Im Rahmen der laufenden Weiterentwicklung der Vertrauensinfrastruktur wurde ein weiterer Schritt erreicht.*
- ✅ *Basisregister ist offen für Eintragungen* · 🚫 *Neues zum Basisregister*
- Do not announce intentions as events. A decision to plan something is not a milestone (OPS-04).

**EDT-02 Say what the reader should do,** or say explicitly that nothing is required. Ambiguity here generates support requests.

---

## 2 Quotes and attribution

**EDT-03 Quote real people, named, with their function.** No composite quotes, no invented enthusiasm.
- A quote adds a perspective the surrounding prose cannot give. A quote that restates the paragraph above it is cut, not shortened.
- SND-01 applies to the sender, not to the speaker. A quoted person may say *we*; the text around them may not.

---

## 3 Newsletters

**EDT-04 The subject line is the most important sentence.** One thing that happened, under 50 characters, not a teaser.
- ✅ *Basisregister offen für Eintragungen* · 🚫 *Das hat sich bei swiyu getan*

**EDT-05 One lead item, then at most three secondary items.** A newsletter with six equal items has no lead.
- No greeting that pretends to a relationship. *Guten Tag* is enough; the salutation rules in the language file apply.

---

## 4 Presentations

**EDT-06 One idea per slide,** stated in the slide title as a full statement, not a label.
- ✅ *Digitale Nachweise senken den Prüfaufwand* · 🚫 *Vorteile*
- Nothing on the slide depends on the speaker. Decks circulate without their presenter; a slide that only makes sense narrated becomes misinformation.
- Every figure on a slide has a source in the notes (OPS-04). Numbers travel further than any other content and are quoted without context.
- The closing slide states what happens next, not *Vielen Dank*.
