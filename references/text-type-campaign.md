# Text type: campaign

Open this file for posters, ads, social posts, claims and campaign landing lines.
- A campaign can address any audience; combine this file with the matching audience row in `SKILL.md` §3.
- Website length budgets do not apply (TYP-02).

---

## 1 What a campaign line has to do

**CAM-01 Every line hits at least one of three goals:**
- **Explain the app** – *It's in your hands* · *The app for my digital credentials*
- **Spark enthusiasm** – *Paperwork without the paper*
- **Build trust** – *100% voluntary* · *decentralised, only on your phone*

**CAM-02 A campaign as a whole hits all three.** A single execution need not.

---

## 2 Who speaks

**CAM-03 Campaign communication may give people a voice.** Elsewhere either swiyu speaks or there is no sender; in campaign work, users may speak for themselves. A quoted enthusiast persuades more than the same sentence as a claim, and puts no words in the Confederation's mouth.

**CAM-04 A quoted voice is a real one.** No composite or invented testimonials. If a quote cannot be sourced, write the line without a speaker.

**CAM-05 Where nobody speaks, SND-01 holds.** No *we*, no *our*.

---

## 3 Licence and its limits

**CAM-06 Metaphor and wordplay are allowed in campaign lines** where they name an established concept. The one text type where they are expected. Body copy under the line stays literal.

**CAM-07 Claim safety is not relaxed.** OPS-04 applies in full. A short line has less room to qualify a claim, so it needs a narrower claim, not a looser one.
- 🚫 *Your data. Nobody else's.* · ✅ *Your credentials stay on your phone.*

**CAM-08 Superlatives stay out** (VOI-07), including implied ones. *Finally* and *at last* imply a verdict the sender cannot make.

**CAM-09 Wordplay must survive four languages.** Test the line in German, French, Italian and English before approval. A pun that works in one language will be replaced in the other three, not translated; decide that consciously.

**CAM-10 No comparison with named alternatives,** commercial or foreign-state (VOI-06).

---

## 4 Form

**CAM-11 One idea per execution.**

**CAM-12 The line works without the image.** A line that only makes sense beside its visual fails in audio, in text-only contexts and in assistive technology.

**CAM-13 A call to action names the next step,** not the emotion.
