# German

Open this file when writing or correcting German. It condenses the Federal Chancellery's Schreibweisungen to what swiyu texts need and records where swiyu departs from them.

---

## 1 Spelling

**DEU-01 Swiss spelling.** *ss* in every position where German spelling would use *ß*. No exceptions.

**DEU-02 Swiss vocabulary** wherever it differs from German usage.
- ✅ *das E-Mail* · 🚫 *die E-Mail*
- ✅ *innert* · 🚫 *innerhalb von*
- ✅ *Konsumentin* · 🚫 *Verbraucherin*
- ✅ *parkieren* · 🚫 *parken*
- ✅ *Spital* · 🚫 *Krankenhaus*
- ✅ *Ferien* · 🚫 *Urlaub*
- Check the Helvetism list in the Schreibweisungen when a word feels German rather than Swiss; do not work through it as a checklist.

---

## 2 Punctuation and typography

**DEU-03 Hyphens with proper names.**
- Ordinary compounds containing a proper name take a hyphen: *Bundes-Register*.
- swiyu product names are the documented exception and take none (NAM-02): *swiyu Wallet*. Product names only.

**DEU-04 Capitals after a colon.**
- A complete sentence after the colon starts with a capital. ✅ *Die neuesten Zahlen zeigen: Die Nachfrage steigt.*
- A single word or an incomplete sentence starts lowercase, unless the word takes a capital for another reason. ✅ *Zuständig sind: das Bundesamt für Justiz und die Bundeskanzlei.*

**DEU-05 Fixed space (Festabstand)** between a figure and what follows it, so the pair never breaks across a line: units, currency, `%`, ordinals, article numbers.

---

## 3 Numbers

**DEU-06 Short numbers in words, longer numbers in figures.**
- One- and two-syllable numbers are written out: *null* to *zwanzig*, then *dreissig*, *hundert*, *tausend*.
- Readability decides where the rule is awkward.

**DEU-07 Several numbers in one context all take figures.**
- ✅ *Die Widerrufsfrist beträgt 7 Tage, bei Finanzdienstleistungen 14 Tage.*

**DEU-08 Figures before abbreviated units, symbols and currencies**: *25 m*, *12 km*, *20 Fr.*, *15 kg*.
- Before a written-out unit, a short number may go either way: *10 Franken* or *zehn Franken*.

**DEU-09 Percentages take figures and a fixed space**: *6 %*, *12,7 %*.
- In non-technical running text a single isolated percentage may be written out: *sechs Prozent*.
- In compounds, no space: *die 0,5‰-Grenze*.
- In derivations, *Prozent* is written out and hyphenated: *4,5-prozentig*.
- *Prozent* takes no plural ending.

**DEU-10 Decimal comma**: *88,5 m*, *12,7 %*.
- **Currency amounts are the exception:** decimal point between unit and subunit. ✅ *Fr. 23.50* · 🚫 *Fr. 23,50*. But *4,16 Millionen Franken*.

**DEU-11 Thousands grouped with a fixed space**: *55 121*, *22 393 518*.
- The apostrophe form is no longer used. Grouping with points or commas is wrong.

**DEU-12 *Million* and *Milliarde* written out in running text.** Abbreviate only in condensed text and tables: *Mio.*, *Mrd.*

## 4 Dates and times

**DEU-13 Years are four digits**: *1999*, *2026*. Never *'26*.

**DEU-14 Year ranges.**
- Two consecutive years: slash, second year shortened, *1870/71*.
- Longer spans: en dash, *1962–1965*.
- With *von*, *bis* is written out: *von 1962 bis 1965*, never *von 1962–1965*.

**DEU-15 Month names are written out in running text**: *am 1. Januar 2026*.
- Abbreviate only in condensed text: *Jan.*, *Febr.* – never *Feb.*

**DEU-16 Weekdays are written out in running text.**
- In tables and forms: two letters, no full stop: *Mo, Di, Mi, Do, Fr, Sa, So*. Three-letter forms are not allowed.

**DEU-17 The 24-hour clock, with a full stop between hours and minutes**, not a colon: *7.33 Uhr*, *14.30 Uhr*.
- The leading zero is dropped, except in lists where *07.33 Uhr* is clearer.

**DEU-18 Ordinals join their word with a fixed space**: *am 20. März*, *das 21. Jahrhundert*.

## 5 Sentence shape

**DEU-25 Keep the verb close to its subject.** Where more than about eight words separate an auxiliary from its participle, split the sentence.

**DEU-26 Avoid nominal style.** A chain of *-ung* nouns is a verb waiting to be released.
- ✅ *Prüfen Sie den Nachweis, bevor Sie Daten weitergeben.* · 🚫 *Vor der Weitergabe der Daten hat eine Prüfung des Nachweises zu erfolgen.*
