# German

Open this file when writing or correcting German. It condenses the Federal Chancellery's Schreibweisungen to what swiyu texts need and records where swiyu departs from them.
- Swiss spelling and vocabulary, abbreviations, dashes and gender are settled in `SKILL.md` (MEC-11, MEC-06, MEC-09, GEN-01) and not repeated here.

---

## 1 Typography

**DEU-01 Hyphens with proper names.**
- Ordinary compounds containing a proper name take a hyphen: *Bundes-Register*.
- swiyu product names are the documented exception and take none (NAM-02): *swiyu Wallet*. Product names only.

**DEU-02 Fixed space (Festabstand)** between a figure and what belongs to it, so the pair never breaks across a line: *25 m*, *20 Fr.*, *6 %*, *am 20. März*, *Art. 7*.

---

## 2 Numbers

**DEU-03 Short numbers in words, longer numbers in figures.**
- One- and two-syllable numbers are written out: *null* to *zwanzig*, then *dreissig*, *hundert*, *tausend*.
- Readability decides where the rule is awkward.

**DEU-04 Decimal comma**: *88,5 m*, *12,7 %*.
- **Currency amounts are the exception:** decimal point between unit and subunit. ✅ *Fr. 23.50* · 🚫 *Fr. 23,50*. But *4,16 Millionen Franken*.

**DEU-05 Thousands grouped with a fixed space**: *55 121*, *22 393 518*.
- The apostrophe form is no longer used. Grouping with points or commas is wrong.

---

## 3 Dates and times

**DEU-06 The 24-hour clock, with a full stop between hours and minutes**, not a colon: *7.33 Uhr*, *14.30 Uhr*.
- The leading zero is dropped, except in lists where *07.33 Uhr* is clearer.

**DEU-07 Weekdays in tables and forms: two letters, no full stop:** *Mo, Di, Mi, Do, Fr, Sa, So*. Three-letter forms are not allowed. Running text writes them out.

---

## 4 Sentence shape

**DEU-08 Keep the verb close to its subject, and prefer verbs to nouns.**
- Where more than about eight words separate an auxiliary from its participle, split the sentence.
- A chain of *-ung* nouns is a verb waiting to be released. ✅ *Prüfen Sie den Nachweis, bevor Sie Daten weitergeben.* · 🚫 *Vor der Weitergabe der Daten hat eine Prüfung des Nachweises zu erfolgen.*
